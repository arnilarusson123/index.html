ABOUT

I'm an Icelandic actor and voice actor based in Reykjavík. I trained at the London Academy of Music and Dramatic Art (LAMDA) and work across Icelandic and international productions on screen, stage and in video games.

Recent screen work includes a starring role in The Darkness directed by Lasse Hallström for CBS, True Detective: Night Country for HBO, and the Netflix feature Against the Ice. I'm currently working on an undisclosed Netflix project.

On stage I've appeared as Malcolm in Macbeth, Bubbi Morthens in 9 Líf, Bjartur in Fyrrverandi, and the title role in Pinocchio — all at Reykjavík City Theatre. My LAMDA credits include Don Carlos, The Cherry Orchard and A Midsummer Night's Dream.

In voice and games I've played Peter Parker / Spider-Man in the Icelandic dub of Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse, Sichfrith in Assassin's Creed Valhalla – Wrath of the Druids, and the Lord of Khorne in Total War: Warhammer III.
